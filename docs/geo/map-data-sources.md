# Data behind the agricultural map

The Situation Monitor's map combines three inputs. Each keeps its own status, and the app never merges them into
one figure. Both shipped assets are built once, offline, by
[`scripts/build_map_assets.py`](../../scripts/build_map_assets.py); the app makes no calls to these services.

| Input | Status | File | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| District outlines | REFERENCE (not verified) | `satquery/agri/assets/manipur_districts_osm.geojson` | ODbL 1.0, © OpenStreetMap contributors |
| Land cover per cell (where farmland is) | SOURCED | `satquery/agmap/assets/landcover_r7.json` | CC BY 4.0, ESA WorldCover |
| Crop types per cell (which crops) | **SAMPLE** (synthetic) | `satquery/agmap/assets/crop_pattern_sample.json` | Generated by LouNgak AI |

Risk is not one of these inputs. It stays the risk engine's per-zone assessment: a cell shows the level of the
monitored zone that holds its centre, and is never scored on its own.

## Display grid

H3 resolution 7 (mean cell about 5.16 km², edge about 1.4 km): every resolution-7 cell whose centre lies inside an
OpenStreetMap district outline, 3,993 cells in all. This is the statewide display grid that
[refined_foundation.md §4.2](../../Context/refined_foundation.md) anticipated. Reports and inspection tasks are
still located by resolution-8 cells; a record belongs to the display cell that is its resolution-7 parent.

## District outlines (OpenStreetMap)

- **Source:** Overpass API, relations `boundary=administrative`, `admin_level=5`, inside the area tagged
  `ISO3166-2=IN-MN`, retrieved 2026-10-31. Relations are matched to the 16 districts in
  `satquery/agri/assets/manipur_district_names.json` by their Wikidata id; neighbouring states' districts that touch
  Manipur are dropped.
- **Processing:** each OpenStreetMap way is simplified once with Ramer–Douglas–Peucker at 0.0003° (about 30 m),
  keeping its end points. Rings are then assembled from the simplified ways. Two districts share the same ways, so
  their common border is identical in both outlines and no gaps or overlaps appear. Outer rings are
  counter-clockwise (RFC 7946); coordinates are rounded to 5 decimals.
- **Result:** 16 districts, 9,209 vertices, 183 KB.
- **Checks** (2026-10-31, with the app's own loaders):
  - `load_areas` accepts every feature;
  - the names equal the LGD list;
  - total area is 22,297 km², against 22,288 km² for the Survey of India outlines in
    [district-boundaries-validation.md](district-boundaries-validation.md) (0.04% apart);
  - the bounds are 92.9707, 23.8336 to 94.7452, 25.6921 (SoI: 92.9705, 23.8329 to 94.7449, 25.6920);
  - every demo zone's representative point falls in the district the zone names.
- **Precedence:** verified outlines configured with `SATQUERY_AGRI_DISTRICTS` (the Survey of India file, local only)
  replace these on the map, labelled VERIFIED.
- **Limits:** community-maintained data, simplified. The outlines are map context and orientation only, not a
  verified or legal boundary. Districts are never scored.

## Land cover (ESA WorldCover 2021)

- **Source:** ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200, tiles N24E093, N21E093 and N24E090, read as Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs
  from the public bucket `esa-worldcover.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com` (only the windows over Manipur are
  downloaded).
- **Attribution:** © ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by
  ESA WorldCover consortium. Licence CC BY 4.0.
- **Method:** pixels are counted by class on 0.001° blocks (12 × 12 pixels, about 100 m). Each block goes to the
  cell holding its centre, and shares are stored as whole percentages per cell (tree, shrub, grass, crop, built,
  bare, water, wetland, other). The district stored with each cell is the outline containing the cell's centre.
- **Result:** 3,993 cells, 200 KB. Cropland is at least 5% in 852 cells and at least 20% in 439. By district, the
  valley districts average 48–64% cropland (Bishnupur also shows 17% water: Loktak), and the hill districts 0–9%.
- **Farmland threshold:** a cell is drawn as farmland from 5% cropland (about 26 ha of a cell) up, and the colour's
  strength follows the cropland share (`MIN_CROPLAND` in `satquery/agmap/__init__.py`).
- **Limits:**
  - a 2021 classification, not this season's planting;
  - shifting cultivation (jhum) in the hills is often classed as shrubland or grassland, so hill farmland is
    under-counted;
  - wet valley paddy can be classed as herbaceous wetland.
- **Still to do:** check the cropland class visually against imagery over valley paddy
  ([refined_foundation.md §11](../../Context/refined_foundation.md)).

## Crop types (SAMPLE)

No crop-type map or district crop statistics are loaded yet. Rather than invent figures as if they were real,
the map colours farmland with a **synthetic, illustrative** crop pattern. The pattern is labelled SAMPLE in the
API (`crops.status`), in the legend, on every cell card and in the crop filter. The loader refuses the file
unless its `status` is `SAMPLE`.

- **How it is generated:** each district is given an illustrative profile, either "valley" (Bishnupur, Imphal
  East, Imphal West, Jiribam, Kakching, Thoubal) or "hill". Each crop's share is that profile's weight, varied by a
  smooth, seeded pseudo-random field (features 15–50 km across), then normalised. Crops under 3% are dropped, and a
  cell whose top crop is under 35% is shown as "mixed". The result is deterministic, so the same cell always shows
  the same SAMPLE mix.
- **What it is not:** a survey, a census, district statistics or a satellite crop-type classification. It must
  never be quoted as where a crop is grown.
- **To replace it:** use district crop statistics (Directorate of Economics & Statistics, area by crop) or a
  validated crop-type map. Then change `crop_mix()` in `satquery/agmap/__init__.py` to read that source, and
  change the status from SAMPLE.

## Rebuilding

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python scripts/build_map_assets.py districts   # about 10 s
.venv\Scripts\python scripts/build_map_assets.py landcover   # a few minutes; needs the districts file
.venv\Scripts\python -m pytest tests/test_agmap.py
```

Rebuilding the districts changes the `retrieved` date in each feature's `boundary_source`. Re-run the checks
above, and update this page, when the outlines change.
